About the IQAC

The Internal Quality Assurance Cell (IQAC) of IITM College of Engineering serves as a quality facilitation and monitoring mechanism dedicated to institutional excellence. The Cell promotes continuous improvement in teaching, learning, research, governance and student support through systematic planning, evaluation and adoption of quality enhancement initiatives.
