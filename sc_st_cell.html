About the SC/ST Cell

The SC/ST Cell of IITM College of Engineering is dedicated to promoting equality, inclusion and academic support for students belonging to the Scheduled Caste and Scheduled Tribe communities. The Cell works to ensure equal opportunities, encourages a discrimination-free learning environment, facilitates awareness programmes and provides guidance regarding student welfare and institutional support.
