About Freshers

"Every journey begins with a new beginning."

The Freshers' Party 2025 marked the beginning of an exciting journey for the newest members of the IITM family. Hosted at Hyatt Centric, Janakpuri, the event brought together students, faculty, and distinguished guests to celebrate new friendships, confidence, and talent.

Students enthusiastically participated in ramp walks, performances, music, dance, and interactive activities before concluding the celebration with DJ, lunch, and unforgettable memories that welcomed them into college life.

With each smile and every handshake, Freshers' 2K25 set the stage for a vibrant journey ahead at IITM College of Engineering.
