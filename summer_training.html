Summer Training 2026

A structured bridge between academic learning and practical technical experience

The Training and Placement Cell of IITM College of Engineering organized Summer Training 2026 for BCA and B.Tech students with a focus on industry-oriented technical learning and practical skill development.

Associated with the IBM SkillsBuild ecosystem, the program brings together CETPA Infotech as the training partner and nasscom foundation as the mentoring body. It is intended to help students strengthen practical technical knowledge, problem-solving ability and industry readiness through guided sessions, practical exercises and exposure to industry-relevant skills.

By connecting classroom knowledge with contemporary technology practices, the training supports students in building technical confidence, professional awareness and a stronger foundation for future learning.

Learning beyond the classroom Technical confidence grows when concepts are explored through practical exercises, structured guidance and collaborative discussion.
