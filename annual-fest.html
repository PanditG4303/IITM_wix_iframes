Celebrating Life at IITM

Beyond classrooms and laboratories, IITM's annual celebrations bring students together through creativity, competition, culture and shared experiences. From the vibrant spirit of Sarang to sporting excellence and festive traditions, these moments define campus life.

The Signature Celebration

Sarang

Sarang is the heart of IITM's cultural landscape — a vibrant celebration where students express themselves through music, dance, drama and every form of creative performance. The campus transforms into a stage for talent, bringing together artists, performers and audiences in an electric atmosphere of shared creativity.

From intimate acoustic sets to spectacular stage productions, Sarang showcases the depth of artistic expression that thrives within IITM's student community. It is a testament to the belief that engineering and creativity go hand in hand.

Spirit • Teamwork • Excellence

Annual Sports Meet

The Annual Sports Meet brings together IITM's finest athletes in a celebration of sportsmanship, determination and teamwork. The campus fields and courts come alive with competitive spirit as students push their limits across a wide range of sporting disciplines.

More than just competition, the Sports Meet embodies the values of discipline, perseverance and camaraderie that define the IITM community. Every event is a showcase of the energy and drive that extends beyond the classroom.

Rhythm • Culture • Celebration

Navratri Fest

Navratri at IITM transforms the campus into a vibrant celebration of tradition and togetherness. Students gather in a swirl of colour and rhythm, celebrating through traditional dance forms that echo across the grounds late into the night.

The festival is a beautiful expression of cultural heritage, where the entire campus community comes together to celebrate the spirit of Navratri through music, dance and festive energy that fills the air with joy and unity.

Light • Joy • Togetherness

Diwali Fest

The Festival of Lights brings a warm glow to IITM as students come together to celebrate Diwali. The campus is illuminated with the spirit of togetherness, as the community gathers to share in the joy of one of India's most beloved festivals.

Diwali at IITM is a time when the bonds of campus life shine brightest — a celebration of light, happiness and the shared experiences that make this institution a home away from home for students from across the country.

Celebrating the IITM Spirit

Every celebration becomes a memory, bringing together creativity, culture, sportsmanship and the shared spirit of campus life.
