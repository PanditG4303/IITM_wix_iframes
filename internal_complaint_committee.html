About the Internal Complaint Committee

The Internal Complaint Committee (ICC) is committed to promoting a safe, respectful and inclusive institutional environment for all members of the IITM College community. The Committee provides a confidential platform for addressing complaints, ensures fair inquiry procedures, promotes awareness regarding professional conduct and works towards maintaining dignity, equality and mutual respect across the campus.
