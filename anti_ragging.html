About the Anti-Ragging Committee

The Anti-Ragging Committee has been constituted to ensure a safe, disciplined and inclusive environment for every student at IITM College of Engineering. The committee works proactively to prevent ragging, spread awareness regarding anti-ragging regulations, investigate reported incidents fairly and take immediate disciplinary action whenever required. Every complaint is handled confidentially while ensuring the dignity and safety of all students.
